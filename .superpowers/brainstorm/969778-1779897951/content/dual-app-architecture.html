Sales in Chat — Integration Points

How sales actions integrate with conversations
From a customer chat:
💬 Customer says "I need 50 cups vanilla"
→ Tap [Quick Sale] button in chat
→ Sale wizard opens with customer pre-filled
→ On completion, receipt sent as chat message
→ Stock updated, balance updated
From the Sales tab:
🛒 Tap [New Sale]
→ Pick customer from contacts/chat list
→ Same guided wizard flow
→ Option to share receipt via chat
→ Payment reminder via chat later
Payment follow-up:
👥 View customer balance card
→ Tap [Send Reminder]
→ Pre-composed message sent in chat
→ "Hi ABC, you owe UGX 120K. Kindly pay."
Stock request to office:
📦 Van stock running low
→ Tap [Request Stock]
→ Structured message to office chat room
→ "Need 200 cups vanilla for tomorrow's route"
